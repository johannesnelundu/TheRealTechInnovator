THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

Cybersecurity Awareness

Technology is powerful, but safe habits matter. Keep these basic practices in mind whenever you go online.

🔑 Protect Your Accounts

Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. Never share verification codes with strangers.

📥 Check Downloads

Download apps from trusted sources, review requested permissions and keep your operating system updated.

🧠 Use AI Wisely

Verify AI-generated information, protect private data and use automation responsibly.
